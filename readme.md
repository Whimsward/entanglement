# Entanglement

A novel revision project by Whimsward.

## Structure

It began as a narrative in five parts, bifurcated by a dual interest in what it looks like for a postmodern kid to land in the court of a land drained of itself by tyranny and what it looks like for a tyrannical genius to stumble onto our postmodern world.

Structure was important to the story being told. It was baked into the metaphysical scaffolding of reality as presented by the novel. Inevitability was a specter looming, both thematic and textual, complicated by the question of uncertainty: yes, *something* is inevitable, what was going to be inevitable, actually?

Railing against structure was equally important. A climactic moment hinges around making the point that taking away someone's ability to *plan* doesn't take away their ability to *exert their will*.

## Change

The first draft was completed somewhere around the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic. It's been half a decade since that draft was written. Things have changed. Not everything has.

Revision is about change. Specifically change meant to elevate a work and increase its chances of success: critical, financial, or as an emotive actor on the hearts of those who consume it.

Something I've realized since starting my revision is that David reflects a past version of myself who felt simultaneously untouchable and powerless. Wanting both closeness and separation, always disjointed from his present. I can heighten this by insinuating early that his "purpose" in being entangled with Thrast is to die so that Thrast can be cut loose of the world.

That was where the first draft went in the end.
Now, on the other side of realizing that ideation of my own death is something I will have to combat, albeit thankfully distantly for now, for the rest of my life, I don't think I'm interested in that ending.

Similarly, I've gotten feedback that people want to see more of the world, to experience a rich fantasy that feels more loved in and to observe more weight on then characters.

I don't know if I can do all of that in one project.

So...

### Restructure

Build smaller. Write a robust narrative from David's perspective, with only enticing hints of the broader story weaving itself around him. Tell the story of David's adventures in Veneth, his startling moments back on Earth, and the conclusion on the bridge where he serves as the judge for Thrast.

Then do it again, from another perspective.
